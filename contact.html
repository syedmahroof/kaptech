FAQs

Frequently Asked
Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries from our customers.

What materials are your cable trays available in?
Our cable trays are available in Hot-Dip Galvanised (HDG), Electro-Galvanised (EG), Pre-Galvanised, Stainless Steel 304, Stainless Steel 316, and Powder Coated finishes to suit every environment.
Do you do custom sizes and non-standard specifications?
Yes. We manufacture to custom widths, depths, lengths, and thickness as per your drawings or specifications. Share your requirements via WhatsApp or the inquiry form and we'll confirm feasibility and pricing.
What is your typical lead time for orders?
Standard products are usually dispatched within 3–7 working days depending on quantity. Custom or special orders typically require 7–15 working days. We will provide an exact timeline with your quote.
Do you deliver outside your local area?
Yes. We deliver pan-India through our logistics partners. Freight charges are calculated based on order weight, volume, and destination. Contact us for freight estimates.
What is the minimum order quantity?
MOQ varies by product. Many standard items have no minimum. For custom or special products, an MOQ may apply. Contact us with your requirement and we will advise accordingly.
Are your products compliant with IS / ASTM standards?
Yes. Our products comply with relevant IS, ASTM, and international engineering standards. Certificates and test reports can be provided on request for specific product categories.
